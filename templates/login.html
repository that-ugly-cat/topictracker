TopicTracker

Search, download, and analyse PubMed literature at scale. Track keyword and MeSH term trends, explore co-occurrence networks, and export data for Gephi — all in one web interface.

  • 1
    Search & Download — query PubMed by keyword and year range; full MEDLINE records downloaded automatically.
  • 2
    Content Analysis — keyword, MeSH, author, journal, and lemma trends with normalization and SVG exports.
  • 3
    Interactive Visualization — Bokeh line charts; pick any entity and compare trends across years.
  • 4
    Semantic Networks — keyword co-occurrence tables (nodes, edges, matrix) ready for Gephi.

Developed by Giovanni Spitale, Institute of Biomedical Ethics and History of Medicine, University of Zurich.
